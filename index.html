        How the signal is made, and how it would have done

        Every trading day in the evening, after the Cairo close, the latest price and news are pulled and nine checks are scored: six on the price chart (long-term trend, medium-term trend, MACD momentum, RSI, Bollinger bands, volume), two on the market (EGX30 and the dollar against the pound), and one on the news and company fundamentals. The points are added up. A total of or more is a BUY, or less is a SELL, anything between is a HOLD.

        The replay covers only the six chart checks, because past news and index readings were not recorded. Two completed trades is far too few to prove anything. Read it as a description of how the rules behave: slow, trend-following, and late at tops.

        This tracker is a research aid built on public price data and news. It is not investment advice and it can be wrong. Prices come from Yahoo Finance, which publishes daily figures for this stock and can lag the exchange, so the page shows the latest closing price, updated once a day. Decide for yourself and size positions so that a wrong call is survivable.
